first earthquake-proof mandir in the world

Sitesh Pattanaik

2026/06/08

BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir, Chino Hills, is the first earthquake proof mandir in the world.

It used something called as a “base-isolator technology”. This innovative design allows the temple to withstand seismic activities.

It works by creating a shock-absorber system between the foundation of the building and the building itself.

w/o it: ground -> foundation -> building
w/_ it: ground -> foundation -> base-isolators -> building

Common types of base-isolators

  • lead-rubber bearings
  • friction pendulum bearings

They move and absorb the ground moving and cause much lesser impact on the structure ahead. However, sustained earthquake and/or tall skyscrapers are still susceptible to damage.
